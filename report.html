Key Insights

Volume Decline Across Major Markets

Overall volume dropped 29.5% WoW (636K → 447K). The largest drops were in MYS (−54.6%), MEX (−72.3%), and THA (−42.1%). This appears to be a normalization from W14 spikes rather than organic decline — PHL actually grew 23.9%.

Pass Rate Broadly Stable Despite Volume Shift

The overall pass rate dipped 1.82pp (89.05% → 87.23%), driven primarily by the mix shift away from high-pass-rate MYS traffic. Most countries individually held steady or improved. 7 of 16 major markets improved their pass rate WoW.

USA Triggered >2pp Alert (−3.56pp)

USA dropped from 95.29% to 91.73% with 532 W15 transactions. Combined with a 19.1% volume decline, this warrants investigation into potential SDK version changes or device-mix shifts in the US market.

Emerging Markets Improving

Several emerging markets showed strong improvement: PAK (+5.45pp), BGD (+4.84pp), CHN (+3.67pp), KEN (+3.47pp), EGY (+3.35pp). This could reflect recent model or anti-fraud rule optimizations.

SELECT
  region,
  COUNT(*) as total_txns,
  SUM(CASE WHEN is_idv_passed = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as passed_txns,
  ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN is_idv_passed = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(*), 2) as pass_rate
FROM idv_aai_liveness_details
WHERE pt >= '20260407' AND pt <= '20260413'
  AND CAST(customer_id AS STRING) LIKE '9929%'
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY total_txns DESC;
